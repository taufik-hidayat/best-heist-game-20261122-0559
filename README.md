**The Velvet Vault** — a midnight museum heist in three rooms: sneak past patrolling guards and their vision cones, dodge sweeping cameras (or hack a terminal to kill them), lift paintings, gems and the Crown Jewel, and slip out the green exit before the alarm hits 100%.

Run it: `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080. Move with WASD or arrow keys, hold E to hack a terminal, press Q for a smoke bomb.
